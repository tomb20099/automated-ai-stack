Winner by Use Case

E-commerce: Tidio. Connects to Shopify in 1 click, handles "where is my order" automatically. Saved 11 hours/week in testing.

Local service (plumber, salon): Crisp. £25, books appointments via chat, sends SMS reminders.

SaaS/Tech: Intercom Fin. Expensive but resolves 68% of tickets without human.

3-Step Tidio Setup (15 minutes)

  1. Install Shopify app, connect
  2. Upload FAQ CSV (or paste 10 common questions)
  3. Turn on "Lyro AI" and set handoff to email after 2 fails

ROI Math

Average small business: 40 chats/week × 5 min each = 3.3 hours saved. At £25/hour = £330/month value for £29 cost.
