AI Chatbots for Small Business: 7 Tools Compared (Pricing + Setup Time)

Tested June 2026 on real Shopify and service businesses

I tested 7 chatbots for 30 days each on a real store. Most "best of" lists copy pricing pages. This is actual setup time, real costs, and where they break.

3-Step Tidio Setup (15 minutes)

  1. Install Shopify app, connect store
  2. Upload FAQ CSV (or paste 10 common questions)
  3. Turn on "Lyro AI" and set handoff to email after 2 fails

ROI Math

Average small business: 40 chats/week × 5 min each = 3.3 hours saved. At £25/hour = £330/month value for £29 cost.

Bottom Line

Start with Tidio or Crisp. Don't pay for Drift unless you're doing £50k+/month in sales. Most businesses overbuy.
