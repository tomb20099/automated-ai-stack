Zapier vs Make vs n8n: Honest Comparison After 2 Years Using All Three

Tested on 200+ real automations

Everyone recommends Zapier. I switched to Make, then n8n. Here's when to use each.

Quick Verdict

When to Choose Zapier

  • You need it live in 15 minutes
  • Non-technical team
  • Budget £70+/month is fine
  • 5,000+ app integrations matter

When to Choose Make

  • Best value for money
  • Visual workflow builder
  • Need routers, iterators, complex logic
  • 10k operations for £16 beats Zapier

Setup example: Shopify new order → Create Xero invoice → Send Slack alert → Add to Google Sheet. Takes 20 minutes in Make, would cost 4 Zapier tasks per order.

When to Choose n8n

  • You have a £5/month server
  • Unlimited workflows needed
  • Data privacy (self-hosted)
  • Comfortable with Docker/basic tech

Warning: 2-4 hour initial setup. After that, it's free forever.

My Stack in 2026

Make for 90% of client work (£16/mo). n8n for my own heavy automations (£5 server). Zapier only when client insists.
